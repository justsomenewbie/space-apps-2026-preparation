## Confirmed instruction and recommended direction

**User instruction:** do not use copyrighted images, music or code without permission. A suitable public licence can supply that permission; attribution alone cannot. Record the exact item and permitted uses before incorporation. An accessible webpage, GitHub repository, “royalty-free” label or AI output is not itself clearance.

**Recommendation, not a team decision:** keep the app silent by default as already proposed in FRONTEND_HANDOFF. For the optional demo soundtrack, prefer a short original instrumental made during the permitted event period. A simple self-composed synthesizer cue without third-party samples is the smallest route; AI composition is an alternative after model/output rights and runtime checks. ## Images and code

For images, prefer team-created diagrams/renders or individual NASA assets with checked credit/rights notices. [NASA media guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) generally permits factual educational use with acknowledgement; third-party items, identities and identifiable people require separate consideration. Do not treat every NASA-hosted image or music track as unrestricted, imply endorsement, or label generated art as measured lunar evidence.

For code, keep using the bounded, pinned candidates in DATA_PREPARATION §3 and inspect the actual files, dependency licences, notices and modifications. [GitHub's licensing guide](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository) explains that a public repository without a licence is not general permission to reproduce/distribute/modify it. MIT/Apache/BSD labels do not automatically cover bundled images, music, model weights, data or fonts. Do not relicense a third-party asset under the product's code licence.

**Rechecked for the user's follow-up:** [CLPS Lunar Mission Browser, pinned MIT licence](https://github.com/EmanuelAbasto/CLPS-Lunar-Mission-Browser/blob/7f2cd2756c3667abe2bc4ee78f8eb2a179956b57/LICENSE) permits suitable code reuse with the copyright/permission notice retained. [PolarWindow, pinned Apache-2.0 licence](https://github.com/MdAhbab/PolarWindow/blob/84075e5f36f49ccf22b7100ae3f68a4ac02e62d1/LICENSE) permits suitable reuse with the licence, applicable notices/NOTICE and prominent notices in changed files. These licences supply permission without a separate email. The reviewer read the selected windows.py, rank.ts and data-sources.md; no conflicting file-level licence appeared. This is not approval of entire apps, bundled data/assets or scientific results. Atlas/NavLunar remain reference-only until reuse permission is established, as recorded in DATA_PREPARATION.
