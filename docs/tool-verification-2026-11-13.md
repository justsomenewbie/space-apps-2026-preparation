## No-credit gate

Automatic selection is not spending permission. If credits are forbidden, exclude generation, refinement, texturing, quota-consuming downloads/exports and provider rendering unless the exact operation is verified not to consume credits. “Free plan” does not mean zero consumption. Do not use another authenticated route to bypass an unavailable connector.

Equation's [official reference](https://equation.io/llms.txt) documents the free calculator and public MCP. The unrelated checks used `a = 2`, `y = a x^2`, `z = x^2 - y^2` and invalid `y = (`. The [Spline MCP guide](https://docs.spline.design/generate/spline-mcp-server) and [pricing](https://spline.design/pricing) distinguish MCP-call limits from AI credits; no Spline call was made. Meshy's [balance API](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/balance) is free, while [generation credit rules](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/15643245-when-were-my-meshy-credits-used-or-refunded) require a budget. [WebCAD](https://www.webcad.ca/) lists STEP export at one credit. Its observed build/capture schemas specify no-credit geometry with `skipFea:true`; stress/thermal studies cost two credits each. A later zero-credit test must omit studies, set `skipFea:true`, and never call `export_step`. Recheck these terms at use time.
